## Full Description
Welcome to **Bloom**, the ultimate privacy-first period and menstrual cycle tracking app designed exclusively for you. We believe your health data is yours alone. That's why Bloom operates with zero cloud servers, zero ads, and zero tracking—your data never leaves your control!

🌸 **Absolute Privacy & Military-Grade Encryption**
- **End-to-End Encrypted:** All data is encrypted locally using XChaCha20-Poly1305 + Argon2id PIN hashing.
- **No Cloud Servers:** Bloom never sends your data to any third-party server.
- **SQLCipher Secured:** Even your local app database is strongly encrypted.
- **Biometric App Lock:** Secure the app with a custom PIN, Fingerprint, or Face Unlock using Android Keystore.

🤖 **AI-Powered Cycle Predictions**
- Intelligent algorithms estimate your next cycle length.
- Accurately predict your fertile window and ovulation days.
- Tailored insights to help you understand your body's natural rhythms.

📝 **Detailed Symptom Logging**
- Track your flow intensity, cramps, headaches, breast pain, back pain, and nausea.
- Log sexual activity, mood changes, and keep personal notes.
- Gain deep insights into how your body behaves throughout the month.

🔔 **Smart & Discreet Reminders**
- Set customizable, local notifications.
- Never miss a period prediction, daily pill reminder, or medical appointment.
- All reminders run locally on your device—no external servers pinging you.

🔗 **Secure Partner Pairing**
- Safely share selected cycle information with a partner.
- Encrypted sync through your personal Google Drive ensures nobody (not even us!) can intercept your data.

☁️ **Own Your Backups**
- Bloom syncs encrypted backups solely to your personal Google Drive account.
- Complete data ownership—restore or permanently delete your backups whenever you choose.

No ads. No data selling. No tracking SDKs. Bloom is built to be your safe space. Download today and experience peace of mind while tracking your health.
